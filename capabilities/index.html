In The Zone Studios

In The Zone Studios is a production services company based in Los Angeles. We handle the logistics, crew, locations, and production infrastructure so you can focus on the creative.

What We Handle

Prep

We work with you and your team to put the production together. Budget, schedule, crew, locations, gear, permits.

Production

We're on set with you handling the day to day. Call sheets, coordination, budget tracking, logistics, problem solving.

Wrap

We make sure nothing falls through the cracks after the shoot. Paperwork, returns, payroll, reconciliation, deliverables.
